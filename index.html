Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Minerva ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Minerva, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide Minerva's features:

  • Account information: Name and email address when you sign up.
  • Google Classroom data: When you connect Google Classroom, we access your course list, assignments, and grades via the Google Classroom API. This data is used exclusively to display your academic information inside Minerva.
  • Aspen SIS data: When you connect your school's Aspen instance, we retrieve your courses, grades, and assignments. Your Aspen credentials are never stored on our servers — they are held in your browser session only.
  • Usage data: Anonymized product analytics to improve the experience.

2. How We Use Google User Data

Minerva's use of data obtained from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Data obtained from Google Classroom is used only to:

  • Display your courses, assignments, and grades within Minerva.
  • Calculate GPA analytics and generate personalized study schedules.
  • Provide context to the Minerva AI academic counselor during study sessions.

We do not use Google user data to serve advertising, train AI/ML models, or share with third parties. We do not transfer or sell Google user data to any third party.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Google Classroom data is processed in real time and, where cached, stored for no longer than 24 hours. GPA snapshots may be persisted to our database to power your GPA history chart, but are associated only with an anonymous identifier — not your Google account or email.

You can delete all stored data at any time from your account settings or by contacting us.

4. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. OAuth tokens from Google are stored in secure, HttpOnly cookies and never exposed to client-side JavaScript. We follow industry-standard practices for server security and access control.

5. FERPA Compliance

Minerva follows FERPA guidelines for the protection of student education records. Academic data is accessed solely on behalf of the student and is never disclosed to third parties without explicit consent.

6. Revoking Access

You can revoke Minerva's access to your Google account at any time:

  • Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Minerva.
  • Or disconnect from Dashboard → Settings → Google Classroom → Disconnect.

Upon revocation, we will not access any further Google data. Previously cached data is deleted within 24 hours.

7. Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data, correct inaccuracies, or request full deletion by contacting us. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Minerva is designed for use by students, including those under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is required for the product, and we do not share student data with advertisers or data brokers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email or an in-app notice. Continued use of Minerva after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, contact us at privacy@minerva.app.
